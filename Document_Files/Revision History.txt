	Defaults need to be restored after programming.
	Camera Trip Point range is from 0-100%.
	100% is about 1 amp.
	home will be close to 180 +/- 2 depending on machine size.
	Stops when home is detected.
	rotates about 354 degress from home and stops.

	  continue away from home until the stop is hit.

	- Changed encoder value calculation for TC7. Now based on encoder direction and not button box data.
	- Changed timing of PWMDTY0 and PWMDTY2 for inspection cam. Turns off via interrupts as well as subroutines so encoder actuation is more consistent.
